Clarity over cleverness.

A project matters more to me when it can survive day-to-day use: clear states, reliable data, practical controls, and maintainable structure.

I am still growing as a computer science student, so I value new algorithms, unfamiliar technologies, new responsibilities, and roles that push me forward.
